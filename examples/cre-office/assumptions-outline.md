# 1201 Broadway

Build an office and retail acquisition pro forma for **1201 Broadway**, a 25,000-RSF property in New York, NY.

## Scenario assumptions

- Acquisition date: 2017-12-31
- Exit date: 2022-12-31
- Historical year: FY17A
- Projection period: FY18–FY22, with FY23 as the stabilized year for exit valuation

## Property detail

- Property name: 1201 Broadway
- Location: New York, NY
- Rentable area: 25,000 sq. ft.
- Occupied area: 23,000 sq. ft. across three tenants
- Vacant area: 2,000 sq. ft., held vacant throughout the projection
- Property taxes: 4.0% of acquisition price in FY17A
- Property management fee: 3.00% of EGI

## Acquisition, exit, and debt assumptions

- Acquisition price: $1,000 / RSF, or $25,000,000
- Acquisition costs: 1.0% of acquisition price
- Loan issuance fees: 1.5% of total senior and mezzanine debt
- Senior acquisition LTV: 50.0% of acquisition price
- Senior loan interest rate: 5.0%
- Senior loan amortization period: 25 years (annual amort.)
- Senior loan maturity: 5 years
- Mezzanine acquisition LTV: 10.0% of acquisition price
- Mezzanine cash interest rate: 7.0% of beginning annual mezzanine balance
- Mezzanine payment-in-kind (PIK) interest rate: 3.0%, capitalized annually
- Mezzanine amortization: none
- Mezzanine maturity: 5 years
- Upfront reserve funding: $62,500
- Operating partner equity contribution: 10.0% of total required equity
- Limited partner equity contribution: 90.0% of total required equity
- Exit capitalization rate: 6.00% of FY23 stabilized NOI
- Selling costs: 1.5% of gross sale price

## Operating assumptions

- Common area maintenance (CAM): $5.00 / RSF / year
- Common area utilities: $3.00 / RSF / year
- Insurance: $2.00 / RSF / year
- Replacement reserves: $2.50 / RSF / year
- Turnover vacancy, free rent, tenant improvements, and leasing commissions: $0

## Pro forma structure

- Base rental income should include each tenant's rent plus potential rent on vacant space at office tenant #1's rental rate
- PGR = base rental income less turnover vacancy and free rent, plus expense reimbursements
- General vacancy should offset all potential rent on the 2,000 vacant RSF
- EGI = PGR less general vacancy
- Weight turnover vacancy by the 30.0% non-renewal probability and 6 months of downtime
- Weight free rent, tenant improvements, and leasing commissions by new-lease and renewal probabilities, using the rollover year's rental rate
- Calculate leasing commissions on the rollover year's annual rent multiplied by the 10-year lease term
- Calculate reimbursements using each tenant's occupied RSF divided by total property RSF. Reduce office tenant #2's reimbursements for expected downtime in FY21
- NOI = EGI less operating expenses, including annual replacement-reserve contributions
- Adjusted NOI = NOI less tenant improvements and leasing commissions, offset by capital costs paid from reserves
- Roll reserves forward using beginning reserves plus annual contributions less capital costs funded, capped at available reserves
- Add the FY17A reserve contribution to upfront reserves before the FY18 opening balance
- Cash flow to equity = adjusted NOI less senior cash interest, mezzanine cash interest, and senior principal amortization
- Add mezzanine PIK interest to the debt balance and repay both remaining debt balances at exit
- Debt yield = annual NOI divided by total initial senior and mezzanine debt
- Include interest coverage and DSCR using both NOI and adjusted NOI. Include mezzanine PIK interest in both coverage denominators
- Unleveraged initial investment should include acquisition price, acquisition costs, and upfront reserves. Leveraged initial investment should also reflect loan issuance fees and debt funding
- Exclude unspent replacement reserves from exit cash flows
- Calculate returns at the property and total-equity levels, before asset management fees or partner profit allocations. No partner waterfall is required

- Annual cash flow to equity, debt balances, debt yield, interest coverage, and DSCR

- Leveraged and unleveraged IRR, total investment, total return, and MOIC
